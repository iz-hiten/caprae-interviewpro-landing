Features

What your team
works with.

  1. 1Recorded video interviewsBuild an interview for each role, then send each candidate their own link. They answer on video, in the browser, whenever it suits them.
    • Job title, type and description: full-time, part-time, internship, contract or freelance
    • An intro video recorded by your team
    • Your own questions, each with its own thinking time and answer time
    • One link per candidate, open for 7 days by default, or bulk invite a list
    • Prefer a conversation? Live interviews send a booking link with the next interviewer in the rotation
  2. 2Transcripts and AI evaluationEach answer arrives already read. Skim the transcript and the notes, then watch the answers that matter.
    • A transcript for every answer
    • An AI score out of 100 for each answer, with strengths and weaknesses
    • The average shown on the interview card as an AI score out of 5
  3. 3Scoring that means the same thingEvery reviewer scores the same three things. InterviewPro weights them and sets the candidate's status.
    • Preparation, communication and mission, each from 0 to 10
    • Weighted 40, 35 and 25 into one final score
    • 8 or more: Selected. Under 5: Rejected
    • Who scored, and when, is saved with the score
  4. 4Weekly ApplicantsPick a date range and see everyone who applied, with each stage of their application in one row.
    • Position, interview type, handbook status and grade, interview status, NDA status and date applied
    • Search across every column; filter by interview type and handbook status
    • Add an applicant by hand, or import Excel or CSV
    • Send the next stage's email from the same row
  5. 5NDA and onboardingSelected candidates are listed with their scores and recordings, ready for the NDA. Accepted candidates move to Onboarding.
    • Send the NDA to one candidate or to everyone selected
    • The dates the NDA was sent and received
    • Search both lists

Emails

The emails it writes for you.

Preview and edit each one before it goes, to one candidate or to everyone you've selected, with each name filled in.

InviteSends the candidate their interview link.
RemindersThree templates: 48 hours, 24 hours and the day itself.
NudgeFor an interview that was started but not finished.
Thank youOnce the answers are in.
ExpiredWhen the link has closed.
RejectedA clear answer for candidates who weren't selected.
CongratulationsFor selected candidates.
Stage emailsHandbook pass or fail, interview invite, interview pass or fail, and the NDA request, sent from the Weekly Applicants table.
