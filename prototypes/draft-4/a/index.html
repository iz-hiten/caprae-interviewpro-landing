How it works

Four steps
to a shortlist.

From the moment someone applies to the moment your team decides.

  1. 1ApplyApplications land in your Weekly Applicants table. You can also add people by hand or import a spreadsheet.
  2. 2HandbookEach applicant completes the handbook for their role. Your team grades it pass or fail.
  3. 3Recorded interviewThose who pass get their interview link and record their answers in the browser.
  4. 4Review and scoreRead the transcript and AI notes, watch what matters, and score. Your score sets the outcome.

Questions

Common questions.

Something else? Ask the team.

Do candidates need an account?

No. Each candidate gets one link. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to install.

How long does an interview link stay open?

7 days by default. Reminder emails are ready for 48 hours before, 24 hours before and the day itself.

Can we use our own questions?

Yes. Each job has its own questions and intro video, and you set the thinking time and answer time for every question.

Can we still run live interviews?

Yes. Choose a live interview for any applicant and they get a booking link with the next interviewer in the rotation.

Does the AI decide who passes?

No. It transcribes each answer and writes an evaluation with a score. A candidate only becomes Selected or Rejected when someone on your team scores them.

Can we bring the applicants we already have?

Yes. Import them from Excel or CSV using the template, or add them one by one.
