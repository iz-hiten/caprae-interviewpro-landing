How it works

Seven steps

  1. 1ApplicationApplications reach the Weekly Applicants table from your inbox. Your team can also add someone by hand or import a spreadsheet.CandidateAutomatic
  2. 2HandbookEach applicant receives the handbook for their role, with a deadline, a short tutorial and an info session. Your team grades it pass or fail; some roles have a second handbook.CandidateYour team
  3. 3InterviewPassing the handbook sends the candidate their recorded-interview link. Choose a live interview instead and they get a booking link with the next interviewer in the rotation.CandidateAutomatic
  4. 4ReviewEach answer arrives with a transcript and an AI evaluation: a score out of 100, strengths and weaknesses. The interview card shows the average out of 5.AutomaticYour team
  5. 5ScoreReviewers score preparation, communication and mission from 0 to 10, weighted 40, 35 and 25. A final score of 8 or more is Selected; under 5 is Rejected.Your team
  6. 6NDASelected candidates get the NDA form, one at a time or all at once. When a candidate accepts it, InterviewPro records the response.Your teamCandidate
  7. 7OnboardingAccepted candidates move to Onboarding, with the dates the NDA was sent and received.Automatic
