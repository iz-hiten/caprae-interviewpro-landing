The problem

The first round is where hiring time goes.

  1. 01

    Every first call needs a slot.

    Two calendars to line up for every applicant, and most of those calls only tell you who isn't a fit.

  2. 02

    The process is in five places.

    Applications in an inbox, notes in a spreadsheet, interviews in a calendar, paperwork in email. Someone always falls through.

  3. 03

    Every interviewer judges differently.

    Two people, two ideas of "good". Without a shared scale, a yes from one reviewer doesn't mean a yes from another.

How it works

Four steps to a shortlist.

  1. 1

    Apply

    Applications land in your Weekly Applicants table. You can also add people by hand or import a spreadsheet.

  2. 2

    Handbook

    Each applicant completes the handbook for their role. Your team grades it pass or fail.

  3. 3

    Recorded interview

    Those who pass get their interview link and record their answers in the browser.

  4. 4

    Review and score

    Read the transcript and AI notes, watch what matters, and score. Your score sets the outcome.

Features

What your team works with.

One link per candidate

No account and nothing to install. Send one link, or invite a whole list at once.

Interviews built per role

Your intro video, your questions, and a thinking time and answer time for each one.

Transcripts and AI notes

Every answer is transcribed, with an AI score out of 100 and its strengths and weaknesses.

Scoring on three things

Preparation, communication and mission, from 0 to 10, weighted into one final score.

Weekly Applicants

NDA to onboarding

Questions

Common questions.

Do candidates need an account?

No. Each candidate gets one link. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to install.

How long does an interview link stay open?

7 days by default. Reminder emails are ready for 48 hours before, 24 hours before and the day itself.

Can we use our own questions?

Yes. Each job has its own questions and intro video, and you set the thinking time and answer time for every question.

Can we still run live interviews?

Yes. Choose a live interview for any applicant and they get a booking link with the next interviewer in the rotation.

Does the AI decide who passes?

No. It transcribes each answer and writes an evaluation with a score. A candidate only becomes Selected or Rejected when someone on your team scores them.

Can we bring the applicants we already have?

Yes. Import them from Excel or CSV using the template, or add them one by one.
