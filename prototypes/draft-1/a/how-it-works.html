How it works

From application to first day, step by step.

Seven steps, and who does what at each one: the candidate, your team, or InterviewPro on its own. Every picture is the real app with demo data.

CandidateYour teamAutomatic
  1. 01
    CandidateAutomatic

    Application

    Applications reach the Weekly Applicants table from your inbox. Your team can also add someone by hand or import a spreadsheet.

  2. 02
    CandidateYour team

    Handbook

    Each applicant receives the handbook for their role, with a deadline, a short tutorial and an info session. Your team grades it pass or fail; some roles have a second handbook.

  3. 03
    CandidateAutomatic

    Interview

    Passing the handbook sends the candidate their recorded-interview link. Choose a live interview instead and they get a booking link with the next interviewer in the rotation.

  4. 04
    AutomaticYour team

    Review

    Each answer arrives with a transcript and an AI evaluation: a score out of 100, strengths and weaknesses. The interview card shows the average out of 5.

  5. 05
    Your team

    Score

    Reviewers score preparation, communication and mission from 0 to 10, weighted 40, 35 and 25. A final score of 8 or more is Selected; under 5 is Rejected.

  6. 06
    Your teamCandidate

    NDA

    Selected candidates get the NDA form, one at a time or all at once. When a candidate accepts it, InterviewPro records the response.

  7. 07
    Automatic

    Onboarding

    Accepted candidates move to Onboarding, with the dates the NDA was sent and received.

Automatic

What happens without anyone clicking.

The steps above marked Automatic, in one list.

  • New applicationsarrive in the Weekly Applicants table from your inbox.
  • A handbook passsends the candidate their recorded-interview link.
  • A live interviewgoes to the next interviewer in the rotation, so no one gets them all.
  • Every answeris transcribed and given an AI evaluation.
  • A saved scoresets the status: Selected at 8 or more, Rejected under 5.
  • An accepted NDAmoves the candidate to Onboarding with the dates recorded.
