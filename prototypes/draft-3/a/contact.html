About

Built by Caprae Capital, for its own hiring.

Caprae Capital provides end-to-end, white-labeled private equity services for buyers of small and mid-size businesses. It was founded in 2025 by Kevin Hong and Zackary Beckham, and is based in Glendale, California.

InterviewPro is the platform Caprae built to recruit its own team. Its steps are Caprae's steps: a handbook before the interview, scores on preparation, communication and mission, then the NDA and onboarding.
