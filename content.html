2. The first round is where hiring time goes.

The problem

The first round is where hiring time goes.

01 Every first call needs a slot. Two calendars to line up for every applicant, and most of those calls only tell you who isn't a fit.

02 The process is in five places. Applications in an inbox, notes in a spreadsheet, interviews in a calendar, paperwork in email. Someone always falls through.

03 Every interviewer judges differently. Two people, two ideas of "good". Without a shared scale, a yes from one reviewer doesn't mean a yes from another.

7. Common questions.

Questions

Common questions.

Do candidates need an account?

No. Each candidate gets one link. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to install.

How long does an interview link stay open?

7 days by default. Reminder emails are ready for 48 hours before, 24 hours before and the day itself.

Can we use our own questions?

Yes. Each job has its own questions and intro video, and you set the thinking time and answer time for every question.

Can we still run live interviews?

Yes. Choose a live interview for any applicant and they get a booking link with the next interviewer in the rotation.

Does the AI decide who passes?

No. It transcribes each answer and writes an evaluation with a score. A candidate only becomes Selected or Rejected when someone on your team scores them.

Can we bring the applicants we already have?

Yes. Import them from Excel or CSV using the template, or add them one by one.

3. Five screens, start to finish.

How it goes

Five screens, start to finish.

1 Upload your resume Drag it in or click to choose it. It's saved for the hiring team to review.

2 Check your camera and microphone You'll see yourself, and a meter shows your sound level as you speak. If the camera doesn't show, use Retry Camera.

3 Watch the team's introduction A short video from the hiring team explains the interview before your first question.

4 Answer each question Watch the question, then take your thinking time; a countdown shows how long you have. Recording starts when it ends, or sooner if you click Start Recording. It stops when your answer time runs out, or when you click Stop. A few seconds later your answer uploads and the next question begins.

5 You're done A thank-you screen confirms your interview is complete. The hiring team reviews your answers and gets back to you.

4. Common questions from candidates.

Questions

Common questions from candidates.

Can I record an answer again?

No. Each answer is recorded once, so use your thinking time to plan it.

How long are the thinking time and answer time?

The hiring team sets them for each question. Unless they change them, you get 30 seconds to think and 2 minutes to answer. The countdown on screen always shows what's left.

How long does my link stay open?

7 days by default. The hiring team may send you a reminder before it closes.

My link says "Interview Not Available". What now?

The link has expired, has already been used, or was copied incorrectly. Try refreshing the page, check you used the full link, and if it still doesn't work, ask the team that sent it for a new one.

Who sees my answers?

The hiring team that invited you. Your answers are transcribed and an AI writes notes on them for the team, but a person on the team makes the decision.
