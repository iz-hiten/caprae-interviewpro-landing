# Buyer lens: what the person buying InterviewPro would love

Written before Draft 1 so the copy sells the **use case**, not the app. Every promise on the
right maps to something the code really does (see `content.html` for sources).

## Who is buying
A partner, founder or principal at a small finance firm: a search fund, an independent sponsor,
a small PE firm or deal boutique. They hire interns and junior associates in batches, have no HR
department, and do the interviewing themselves on top of deal work. Caprae Capital is exactly this
buyer: it built InterviewPro to hire its own PE, buyside, research, marketing and software interns.

## Their week today (the pain, in their words)
- "Applications pile up in an inbox, and I'm copying names into a sheet at night."
- "I give 30 minutes to a first call and know in two that it's a no."
- "My partner and I interview the same person and walk away with different opinions."
- "Good candidates go quiet because we took a week to reply. Some never hear back at all, and that's on our name."
- "The NDA goes out by email, and I chase it."

## What they'd love (and what makes it true)
| They'd love… | Because InterviewPro… |
|---|---|
| **Live time only with people who've already shown they can think and talk** | gives every applicant a handbook, then recorded answers, before anyone books a call |
| **Reviewing in minutes, not hours** | transcribes every answer and adds AI notes (score, strengths, weaknesses), so they skim first and watch what matters |
| **One standard across the team** | scores everyone on Preparation, Communication, Mission (40/35/25) and shows who scored |
| **Never being the firm that ghosts** | sends invites, reminders, thank-yous and decisions from one place, one at a time or in bulk |
| **Candidates who think "this firm is serious"** | opens with an intro video from the team, gives thinking time before each answer, and confirms by email |
| **Staying in charge** | lets the AI suggest but only moves a candidate when a person scores |
| **Paperwork that finishes itself** | sends the NDA in one click and moves signers to Onboarding |
| **No new system to babysit** | one page per role to set up; one table for every applicant |

## How it should feel to read
Calm, confident, a little exclusive: like the firm they want to look like to candidates.
Short sentences. Their words ("first call", "the few worth meeting"), not ours ("pipeline
automation", "AI-powered"). Numbers only where we have them (Caprae's, labelled as Caprae's).

## The spine of the copy
**Interview candidates on video. Meet the ones who stand out.** Candidates record their answers
from one link; the team reads transcripts and AI notes, scores on three things, and moves the
best people from handbook to NDA in one dashboard. (Not "every applicant": in the real pipeline
applicants do the handbook first, and only those who pass are interviewed.)

## How the videos are used (Draft 1, rebuilt)
A buyer wants to see the real product, not an illustration of it. So every moving picture on the
site is **the actual InterviewPro app**, run locally with made-up data (Candidate 1…73,
example.com emails, a silhouette on camera) and screen-recorded in Chrome; nothing is redrawn.
How it was made and how to re-record: `tools/recording/README.md`.
- **Home**: the problem, four steps, six features, what sets it apart, who built it, FAQ. Stills of the real app, no video.
- **Features**: each feature with a still of the app and what it does, then the emails and team roles.
- **How it works**: who does what at each of seven steps, then what happens on its own.
- **For candidates**: what a candidate needs and sees, screen by screen; the buyer can send this page with each link.
- **About & contact**: who Caprae Capital is, why it built InterviewPro, and the form.
- Every video plays only while on screen, can be paused, and shows its poster frame for people
  who turn motion off. Each is labelled "Real app · demo data".
